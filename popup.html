Off unless you turn it on. When on, LeetSync sends a random ID, the extension version, your theme, which features you use, and every submission result — problem, difficulty, language, verdict, runtime and memory. It never sends your token, repository, or GitHub username. Turning it off deletes the queue and forgets the ID. Privacy policy ↗
